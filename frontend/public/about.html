Product Image Finder

Turn a product list into a library of images you can review and export. Product Image Finder is a browser app and installable PWA with a private workspace for each account.

Open your catalogue studio

From spreadsheet to approved images

  1. Upload your list. Start with a CSV or Excel file containing product names and pack sizes.
  2. Find product photos. The app searches supported public retailer catalogues and offers image candidates where listings are available.
  3. Review every match. Check the brand, variant, pack size and image quality. Size mismatches and quality warnings remain visible. Upload your own photo when needed.
  4. Export your catalogue. Download approved images as a ZIP and product reports as CSV or Excel.

Search coverage depends on the available source catalogues. A result is a candidate for your review, not a guarantee of an exact match or permission to reuse an image.

Private accounts and an installable app

Sign in with your email and password, or choose Owner access. New customers need a private invitation link sent by the owner to their email address. Each customer sees their own catalogues.

On supported browsers, choose Install app. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share menu and Add to Home Screen. Internet access is required for searching and saving catalogue work; keep the app open while searches run.
